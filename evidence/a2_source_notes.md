# ISE-A2 Source Notes

These sources provide public context for appointment scheduling and personal-information/security boundaries. They do **not** establish the baseline or policy of a specific clinic that has not yet been selected.

1. **Who Misses Appointments Made Online? Retrospective Analysis of the Outpatient Department of a General Hospital in Jinan, Shandong Province, China.** *Journal of Medical Internet Research* (2020). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7708679/ . The paper analyses 48,777 online appointment records from a particular hospital and reports a 15% no-show rate in that dataset/time period. Do not generalise that value to all Chinese clinics or claim it is the target clinic's baseline.
2. **Effects of a comprehensive reservation service for non-emergency registration on appointment registration rate, patient waiting time, patient satisfaction and outpatient volume in a tertiary hospital in China.** *BMC Health Services Research* 19, 782 (2019). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6824126/ . This is evidence that reservation design and outpatient workflow have been studied in a Chinese tertiary-hospital setting; the effects cannot be assumed to transfer unchanged to a small clinic.
3. **Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China**, particularly Articles 28–30 on sensitive personal information. English translation hosted by the Supreme People's Procuratorate: https://en.spp.gov.cn/2021-12/29/c_948419_2.htm . Confirm applicability with qualified local guidance before production deployment.
4. **Measures on Cybersecurity Management for Medical and Health Institutions** (official Chinese government publication, 2022). https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/202208/31/488953/article.html . Context for institutional security governance; this coursework model is not a compliance assessment.
5. **Guiding Opinions of the General Office of the State Council on Promoting the Development of “Internet + Healthcare”** (2018). https://english.www.gov.cn/policies/latest_releases/2018/04/28/content_281476127312948.htm . Policy context only; not evidence that a particular workflow is deployed in the target setting.
