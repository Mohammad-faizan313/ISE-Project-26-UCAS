# Source verification notes

Evidence in the report was checked against the publisher/index records and official National Health Commission pages on 2 October 2026.

## Source types
1. Official Chinese government policy/statistics: NHC notices and press briefing.
2. Peer-reviewed empirical and modelling research: PubMed-indexed / Springer / BMC records for Chinese outpatient settings.

## Important interpretation rule
The 15% no-show rate is a published result from a 2017-2018 Jinan general-hospital online-appointment dataset. It is **not** presented as a measurement of the target clinic.

The 17% web-system usage result is from one Shanghai hospital survey in 2014, and the 63.4% appointment-system usage result is from a 2012 West China Hospital survey. These older figures are used to demonstrate context dependence and adoption barriers, not to claim current national behaviour.

The 2024 NHC statistic reports that 82.7% of secondary-and-above public hospitals had appointment services and that tertiary public hospitals had a 61.1% average appointment rate. This is cited as a dated official measurement, not as a current 2026 census.
